    How MediaFit decides

    1. LookIs it a photo (many colours, soft details) or a graphic (flat colours, sharp text)? Does it have transparent parts?
    2. Choose a formatPhotos try JPEG and WebP and keep the smaller supported candidate. Graphics use PNG to preserve pixels. WebP candidates are labelled as lossy, including at maximum quality.
    3. Fit the goalResize to the longest side of your goal. If the file is still over the limit, lower the quality step by step, then the size.
    4. Check qualityEstimate brightness similarity on copies up to 800 px. SSIM does not fully measure colour, transparency or detail lost by resizing. Inspect the preview too.

    THIS BROWSER

    Capability check

      Using MediaFit

      What is the best way to start?

      Try the built-in examples. For images, choose your goal and inspect Format lab. Use Edit for individual settings. For video, try Balanced first and compare the measured output size.

      Why did the file get larger?

      A different format, higher bitrate or resizing a highly optimized graphic can produce a larger file. Image Auto keeps the original when it meets all constraints and is smaller. Video exports show the measured change; keep the original if it is better.

      Does quality 80 mean 80% quality?

      No. Quality is a setting of a specific encoder. Equal numbers across JPEG and WebP do not imply equal perceptual quality. Compare important details visually.

      What does SSIM measure?

      Our image estimate compares luminance on resampled copies up to 800 px. It does not fully measure colour, transparency or original-resolution detail. No SSIM score is calculated for video.

      Video & compatibility

      Why does video take time?

      The browser re-encodes frames while playing the selected portion. A 30-second clip takes roughly 30 seconds plus preparation. Keep this browser tab in the foreground; switching away pauses encoding until you return.

      Can I export MP4?

      MP4 appears only when your browser supports recording the required codecs. WebM is available in supported browsers. Input playback and output recording have separate compatibility requirements.

      Is the estimated size guaranteed?

      No. Bitrate is a target; content, encoder behaviour, audio and container overhead affect the final size. Check the measured result before downloading.

      What are the limits?

      Images: 60 MB and 40 megapixels. Videos: 150 MB, 5 minutes and at most 3840 × 2160 equivalent input pixels. Animation conversion and HDR preservation are not supported. Canvas export can remove metadata and alter colour profiles. Unsupported or encrypted codecs cannot be decoded.
